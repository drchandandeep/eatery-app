This policy explains what information the Kahumbo app collects, how it's used, and who can see it. Kahumbo is a food ordering platform connecting local stores with nearby customers.

Information we collect

  • Customers: name, email address, phone number, delivery address, GPS location (used once at signup to confirm you're within a store's delivery range), and your order history.
  • Store owners: name, email address, phone number, store address, GPS location of the store, and payment QR code images you upload.
  • Payment information: Kahumbo does not process payments directly or store card/bank details. Payments are made via UPI, directly between the customer and the store (or platform, for subscriptions), outside the app. We only store whether an order/subscription was marked as paid, plus any payment screenshot you choose to upload as proof.

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match customers with nearby stores and enable ordering
  • To send order confirmations and updates (if you've provided an email)
  • To verify store subscription payments

Who can see your information

A customer's name, delivery address, and order details are visible to the store they order from, so the order can be fulfilled. A store's location and menu are visible to nearby customers. Platform administrators can see store subscription payment proofs for verification purposes.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
